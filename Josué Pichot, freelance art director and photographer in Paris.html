I’ve enjoyed drawing since I could barely hold a pen.
Later, classmates were paying me to make their artwork.
As a teenager, I was that friend who always took pictures with a disposable camera and made a wall of all the prints.
Graffiti shook my world before anyone called it street art.
I’m a middle-class dude from the Paris suburbs: hip hop is my culture.

I started my studies at Gobelins learning Flash and Director. Well, that’s how old I am.
My last studies at Gobelins were about mastering flash lighting for studio photography.

I created and ran a digital creative studio for 15 years, working for renowned music artists, blockbusters, platforms, luxury, lifestyle, start-ups and food & beverage brands.
I aim to create impactful, engaging and emotionally resonant work. From advertising campaign writing to branding and graphic design, I ensure each deliverable reflects meticulous attention to detail and quality, whether I’m personally involved in execution or collaborating with other talented individuals.

About my day rate: it will be simpler if you give me your budget, and I’ll tell you if I fit in.
(I work with “big” clients and small ones, the proof is on my Insta.)
